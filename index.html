About our company

Melbourne, VIC · Family owned

Global IT Technology is an Australian family-owned business founded in Melbourne. Our founder and director brings 20 years' experience in telecommunications and technology as a business analyst, project manager and system administrator across different industries.

She founded Global IT Technology to build an Australia-based company that, unlike other IT firms, could offer genuinely flexible services backed by a highly skilled team.

“Our mission is a trustworthy relationship — never overpriced gear you don't need.”

Having watched clients pressured into buying overpriced items they did not necessarily need, the company's mission is simple: earn trust first, then solve the problem properly.
